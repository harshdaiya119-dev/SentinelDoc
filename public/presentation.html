CRITERION 1 // PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING THREAT LANDSCAPE

Deconstructing the PII Crisis & The 'Fake Redaction' Flaw

01 // THE LEAKAGE EPIDEMIC

Unchecked Document Exposures

Over 82% of enterprise data breaches originate from accidental internal document sharing. Millions of unredacted Aadhaar, PAN, and credit cards escape via emails, support tickets, and open cloud buckets daily without pre-flight security checks.

02 // THE FAKE REDACTION FLAW

The Optical Illusion of Privacy

Most commercial tools simply draw black rectangles on the visible UI layer. The underlying text stream remains 100% selectable, copy-pasteable, and scrapeable. Organizations falsely assume documents are sanitized while remaining completely exposed.

03 // STATUTORY PENALTIES

DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR Mandates

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 imposes statutory penalties up to ₹250 Crore ($30M+) per breach event. Manual redaction is slow, cost-prohibitive, and vulnerable to human fatigue.

Multi-Format Deep Sanitization

  • • DOCX XML Reconstruction: Run-level decomposition scrubs text while preserving tables, fonts, and layouts.
  • • Spreadsheets (CSV/XLSX): Cell coordinate masking preserves formulas and formatting.

ARCHITECTURAL RATIONALE FRAMEWORK SELECTION

Technology Stack & Frameworks: What We Used & Why

BACKEND CORE & DETECTION

FastAPI & Microsoft Presidio

  • • FastAPI (Python 3.9): High async throughput, automated Pydantic schema validation, and zero-latency in-memory non-blocking stream processing.
  • • Microsoft Presidio & spaCy: Industry-standard PII detection combining spaCy NER with regex patterns for maximum recall on unstructured text.
  • • PyMuPDF (fitz): Low-level binary PDF stream redaction (apply_redactions) physically purges glyph objects, eliminating visual-only fake redactions.
COMPUTER VISION & DOCUMENT I/O

OpenCV, Vision OCR & Document Parsers

  • • OpenCV & Haar Cascades: Zero-latency local face detection to auto-crop cardholder photos & built-in QRCodeDetector with zero cloud calls.
  • • macOS Vision OCR: On-device neural OCR ensures 100% data sovereignty; never exposes confidential ID documents to external cloud APIs.
  • • python-docx & openpyxl: Direct XML run & cell-coordinate sanitization without corrupting Word styles or Excel formulas.
  • • ReportLab: Programmatically compiles signed, tamper-evident forensic PDF audit certificates.
